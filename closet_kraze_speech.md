### [Section 1: The Scarcity Mechanism (Why Real Scarcity Rules)]

Human beings do not value what is abundant. They value what is slipping through their fingers.

On Closet Kraze, scarcity isn't a clever marketing trick or a fake countdown timer. It is hardcoded into our infrastructure. 

- **Drop Timers & Scheduled Releases**: Products do not sit on virtual shelves collecting digital dust. They are locked behind a ticking clock. When that timer hits zero, the gates lift. 
- **Real-Time Inventory Lock**: When a user selects their size, those items are locked in real-time. If you hesitate, someone else in the active queue takes it. The screen shows you exactly how many pairs are remaining in your region—whether there are 3 left, 2, or 1.
- **Sold-Out Artifacts as Social Proof**: When a collection is gone, we don’t hide it. We preserve the sold-out items on our grid. Why? Because sold-out artifacts are the ultimate social proof. They tell the latecomers: *"You missed out. Next time, move faster."*
