# Refund, Shipping, and Cancellation Policy (Closet Kraze)

## 1. Cancellation Policy

### Pre-Dispatch
- You may cancel an order anytime before the fulfillment chamber initiates dispatch (typically within 24 hours of acquisition). A full refund will be initiated immediately.

### Post-Dispatch
- Once a tracking protocol is active, the order cannot be canceled. You must initiate a return sequence upon delivery.

### System Cancellations
- We reserve the right to terminate anomalies—including inventory discrepancies or suspected fraudulent activity. You will be fully refunded if a system cancellation occurs.

---

## 2. Shipping Policy

### Delivery Times
- **Standard Shipping:** 3-10 business days.
- **Processing Time:** All orders are sanitized and prepared for dispatch within 24-48 hours.

### Tracking
- Real-time neural tracking link provided via the Communication Terminal once the dispatch sequence initializes.

---

## 3. Refund and Return Policy

### Return Window
- **5 Days Return Protocol:** You have 5 days from the moment of physical delivery to initiate a return sequence.

### Conditions for Return
- Item must be in **Original Vault Condition** (unworn, unwashed, all original tags and security seals intact).
- Original packaging must be preserved.

### Non-Refundable Items
- Underwear and intimate apparel.
- Beauty and skincare products (if seal is broken).
- Custom-modified silhouettes (Style Protocols).
- Limited "Final Drop" items marked as high-scarcity.

### Refund Process
- **Inspection:** Once received at our central node, items undergo a 24-hour aesthetic inspection.
- **Approval:** If approved, a refund is triggered to your original funding source (Paystack gateway).
- **Velocity:** Credits typically materialize within 3-5 business cycles depending on your banking institution.
