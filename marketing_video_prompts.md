# Closet Kraze: Curated Marketing & Video Ad Scripts (7 Concepts)

This document contains **7 high-fidelity video prompt scripts** optimized for short-form social video advertising (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) and cinematic brand announcements. Each script features specific camera setups, key styling/vibe cues, and human-centric messaging designed to capture the essence of high-fashion techwear and streetwear.

---

## 📅 Concept 1: "The Vault Decrypted" (Hypemode Reveal)
*   **Format:** 15-second high-energy teaser
*   **Objective:** Introduce user to Clozet Kraze elite, gated archive drops.
*   **Target Channel:** TikTok / Instagram Stories

```markdown
[0-3s] VISUAL: High-contrast close-up of a neon blue vault wheel starting to rotate and spin with mechanical locking sounds. Light beams leak from the seam.
AUDIO: Heavy bass drop with a mechanical "clank".
TEXT OVERLAY: "INTEL SHIELD DECRYPTED."

[3-8s] VISUAL: Rapid-fire 0.5s match cuts of premium technical details—a metallic zipper pull, a matte buckle, raw textured utility cargo straps, glowing embroidery.
AUDIO: Fast-paced electronic synth riser.

[8-12s] VISUAL: A low-angle portrait shot of a model wearing the signature utility windbreaker, gazing clinically at the camera.
VOICEOVER: "The archive is open. Acquire the silhouette before the signal is lost."

[12-15s] VISUAL: The screen cuts to the glowing blue "CLOSET KRAZE" logo with an animated "ENTER THE VAULT" button.
AUDIO: Sub-bass rumble.
```

## 🎨 Concept 2: "The Scroll Experience" (Tactical Shopping)
*   **Format:** 30-second user flow showcase
*   **Objective:** Show the immersive, fluid scroll-mode shopping interface.
*   **Target Channel:** YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels

```markdown
[0-5s] VISUAL: Split-screen style. On the left, a model in crisp, brutalist streetwear takes a sharp turn on a concrete staircase. On the right, a screen capture of a hand instantly scrolling through the smooth "Scroll View Mood" of the Closet Kraze app with fluid transitions.
VOICEOVER: "Stop window-shopping. Experience dynamic style stream matching."

[5-15s] VISUAL: Fast-paced interaction view. The screen tap demonstrates liking a product, adding custom sizes, and watching the price sync with a clean tactical micro-interaction glow.
AUDIO: Smooth, minimalist low-fi house beat kicking in.
TEXT OVERLAY: "Custom Fit & Dynamic Alignment."

[15-25s] VISUAL: Close-up of a finger swiping down. One product morphs flawlessly into the next, showcasing real-time inventory updates and custom-tailored aesthetics.
VOICEOVER: "Unmatched curation velocity. Your next signature silhouette is one swipe away."

[25-30s] VISUAL: High-contrast call to action displaying: "TAP TO ACCELERATE." Direct link to Scroll Mode.
```

## 📸 Concept 4: "Point and Retrieve" (Neural Camera Search)
*   **Format:** 30-second interactive demonstration
*   **Objective:** Promote the new "Camera Visual Search" feature.
*   **Target Channel:** Instagram Reels / UGC Style

```markdown
[0-5s] VISUAL: A host spots someone on the street wearing an incredibly styled techwear cargo jacket.
HOST (to camera): "Excuse me, where did you get that jacket? I need it right now."
PASSENGER: "Honestly, I forgot, it was a limited drop."

[5-10s] VISUAL: Host opens the Closet Kraze app, clicks the "Camera" icon, and focuses the lens on the jacket.
VOICEOVER: "No name? No brand? No worries. Activate Neural Silhouette Scanner."

[10-20s] VISUAL: Screen view showing the live camera capturing the jacket. A blue scanning laser glides over-screen, and matching database fits instant-load with confidence ratings (e.g., "98% Match discovered").
AUDIO: Satisfying futuristic "beep" or sync sound.

[20-25s] VISUAL: Host clicks checkout on the item with a satisfied grin.
HOST (to camera): "Snatched it. Instant retrieval."

[25-30s] VISUAL: Call-to-action screen with logo. "Snap. Match. Secure."
```

## 📦 Concept 6: "The Pack Opening" (Sensory Streetwear Unboxing)
*   **Format:** 45-second satisfying ASMR style video
*   **Objective:** Increase credibility, showcase luxury packaging details and premium textures.
*   **Target Channel:** YouTube / TikTok UGC

```markdown
[0-10s] VISUAL: High-contrast, clean matte-black Closet Kraze delivery box set upon a polished concrete floor. Hands slowly slicing open the security label with a metallic craft knife.
AUDIO: Crisp paper slicing and light card ripping sound. Very close-range high-quality audio.

[10-25s] VISUAL: Lifting the lid to reveal custom charcoal wrapping paper with glowing branding. The model unpeels the custom stickers, showing close-up shots of matte fabric tags, pristine nylon seams, and clean textures.
AUDIO: Satisfying crinkle of high-gsm packing tissue paper.

[25-35s] VISUAL: The model pulls out a sleek oversized hoodie and pulls it on, showing the exact silhouette weight, cozy interior texture, and clean fit.
VOICEOVER (Deep, relaxed tone): "Uncompromising craftsmanship. Delivered straight to your perimeter."

[35-45s] VISUAL: Graphic transition to the app interface, showing how to purchase this exact item. Logo fades in.
```
